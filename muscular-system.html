Overview

Muscle Overview

The muscular system consists of over 600 muscles that comprise approximately 40% of total body weight. These muscles work in coordinated groups to produce movement, maintain posture, and generate heat. The muscular system is divided into three types: skeletal muscles (voluntary movement), smooth muscles (internal organs), and cardiac muscle (heart). Skeletal muscles attach to bones via tendons and work in opposing pairs to create controlled, precise movements throughout the body.
