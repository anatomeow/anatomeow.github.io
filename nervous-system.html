Nervous System

The nervous system is the body’s main communication network, responsible for coordinating actions, processing sensory information, and maintaining internal balance.

Overview

Brain Overview

The brain is the most complex organ in the human body, weighing about 3 pounds and containing approximately 86 billion neurons. It serves as the command center for the entire nervous system, processing sensory information, controlling motor functions, and enabling higher-level cognitive processes such as thinking, learning, and memory. The brain is divided into major regions including the cerebrum, cerebellum, and brainstem, each responsible for specific functions essential for life.
